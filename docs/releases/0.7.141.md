# Studio 0.7.141 — Duration-group video prompt batches

New GPT-5.6 Sol video prompt requests are grouped and processed in this order:

1. SRT duration <= 4 seconds: template_video_4s_prompt.txt
2. 4 < duration <= 6 seconds: template_video_6s_prompt.txt
3. 6 < duration <= 8 seconds: template_video_8s_prompt.txt
4. Duration > 8 seconds: template_video_10s_prompt.txt

Each group sends at most 10 SRT rows per ZIP request (fixed video limit, independent of the image batch-size setting). Larger groups split into 10-row batches and a final remainder. Original scene numbers and within-group SRT order are retained. For example, 23 scenes longer than 8 seconds send 10 + 10 + 3 rows.

Each duration group has its own conversation session. Its video template plus template_zip_file.txt is attached once and reused for subsequent batches while the conversation remains valid. Switching duration groups starts the next instruction session. Image batches follow video groups with their existing settings.

Every returned numbered TXT is validated and saved to the original scene's video prompt. Existing image prompts are retained. Existing queued jobs retain their previous saved grouping; start new jobs for the new batching behavior. Other models keep their existing behavior.

Restart Studio after updating. No Chrome extension update is required.
