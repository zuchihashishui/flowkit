# Studio v0.7.86 — Automatic video duration per SRT scene

Scene Board always sends video generation in SRT duration mode. The shared
manual duration selector is removed, and old saved manual preferences are
ignored. Each Video row shows the duration that will be requested from Flow.

| SRT duration (end minus start) | Video duration |
| --- | --- |
| Up to and including 4 seconds | 4 seconds |
| Over 4, up to and including 6 seconds | 6 seconds |
| Over 6, up to and including 8 seconds | 8 seconds |
| Over 8, up to and including 10 seconds | 10 seconds |

Scenes longer than 10 seconds use 10 seconds and display a shorter-than-SRT
notice. Each scene in the same selection gets its own duration. Existing jobs
keep their saved settings; retry resumes or reuses the original job settings.

Validation: JavaScript syntax checks, 21 scene-board/handoff UI tests and
41 storyboard backend tests passed. Boundary coverage checks exact thresholds
and one millisecond above them, with real imported SRT timestamps and queued
job payloads. No live Google Flow generation was performed.

Update the full source ZIP and restart Studio. No extension reload is needed.
