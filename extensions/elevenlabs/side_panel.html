Automatic tab setup

Before each chunk, the bridge closes all ElevenLabs Text to Speech tabs in this Chrome profile, opens one new tab and binds it automatically. It then clears old text, refreshes the page, enters the chunk and generates speech. Other tabs stay open. Sign in to ElevenLabs first; use one speaker block. Manual Bind tab and Check page are available for inspection, not required to start the queue.
